# Code of Conduct

This project follows a respectful, inclusive collaboration model.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on project goals and quality.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hate speech.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict access for violations.
